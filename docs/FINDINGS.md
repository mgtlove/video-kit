# Findings

Faults seen in rendered frames that are not fixed yet. Each says where it shows, what causes it when known, and what would prove it fixed. A finding leaves the open list in the commit that fixes it and goes to the closed list with the step that fixed it. The `video-frames` skill writes here when it sees something the checker does not measure.

## Open

- F5 (design, not a fault): the explanation card (`card()`, a kicker, a title and a line over the recreated screen) reads as a device inherited from the earlier work rather than a natural part of the video: it interrupts, repeats what the voice says, and its kicker adds a third voice. Seen 5 October 2026 in the first MP4. To judge on the sample video (roadmap step 10), not before: drop the kicker; let the menu choose card, caption or none per video; a caption under the screen may serve better than a card over it. Nothing in the engine changes until the sample video has been looked at. On the S3 test video (9 October 2026) the card's size set the rule of thumb: at 54 px in a 620 px card one line holds about 20 characters and two lines 40, so the expert's Card cells are cut to two lines by the director; on a console at 1:1 or pushed in there is room for one or two lines in most places, and on the Block Public Access screen (text edge to edge at 1.3x and at 1:1) there is no clear spot for a card at all, so that row has a stroke on the control and no card. The engine now finds the nearest clear spot and `cards-clear` proves it; whether the card should make its own room (a lane beside the screen, a caption under it) is this finding's decision.

- F7, the recreated S3 screens set their text in a fallback face: `faces/aws-console.css` in `apps/aws/s3` is empty until `vkit face aws-console --family "Amazon Ember" --weights 400,500,700 --into apps/aws/s3` is run on the Mac with the console open in the capture browser. Proved fixed when the face exists, the rows rise, and the pairs are looked at again.

- F9, one still in about three check runs differs between two browser sessions: 31 pixels by up to 11 levels inside the list page's refresh icon (seen at 0.32 s under the opening fade and at 100.73 s mid camera move, 9 October 2026, S3 test video; the `deterministic` row now names the still). Cause measured: the icon is a crop from the 2x capture drawn at 18 by 21.5 CSS px, and a picture at a fractional size is resampled with a half-pixel edge that the browser rasterizes differently from one session to the next; with the icon at 18 by 22 the flake fell to one run in seven, 9 pixels by one level, on the anti-aliased corner of a card border mid move. Fix to make: `vkit app crop` rounds a region up to whole screen pixels (even width and height at 2x) and says so; the S3 app's fifteen odd-sized crops re-cut and their CSS sizes made whole in `make-states.js` (positions at .5 px stay, they are text); then a check with the row passing ten times running. Open question for Matthew: whether `deterministic` should tolerate one level on a few pixels mid move, as `seek-equals-playback` already tolerates 8 levels on 0.05 percent, or stay byte for byte.

## Closed

- F8, links and marks sat on their text in every S3 state: the `Info` after a heading, the arrow after a link, the caret after the region, placed at the x measured in the capture while the text before them was set in a wider fallback face. Seen 6 October 2026 by Matthew in the first eight side-by-sides. Fixed the same day: everything that follows a text now runs inline after it with the measured gap, so the text's own width carries it in any face; and `vkit check` gained three judged fidelity rows (`state:<id>:overlaps`, `state:<id>:overflow`, `faces`) that name each such fault, proved on a throwaway app (`fidelity.test.js`) and on the S3 states before and after the fix (eight failing rows naming the same places Matthew named, then eight passing).

- F6, a video made with `vkit new --app` for a real app crashed `vkit check` with `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'offsetWidth') at pos (rig.js:48)`: the starter's timeline still named the starter's own screen ids (`r1`, `r4`, `f4`, the state `work-item`), none of which an app's states have, and `focusEl` and `show` reached into a null element while `ink`, `pointer`, `type` and `who` skipped a missing one in silence. Seen 6 October 2026 on the first S3 test video. Fixed in engine 0.4.3: every lookup a beat makes goes through one function that records the miss (`VK.faults()`: the call, the id, the beat's time, the state up) and does nothing else, and `vkit check` reports them in a `beats-on-screen` row that names each. Proof `faults.test.js`: four bad beats in a starter video are recorded once each with their times, the good beats still fire, the row fails and names them; in an app-backed video an unknown state is a recorded miss and shows nothing.
- F1, the explanation card was see-through: `--panel` carried a 93 percent alpha and the fields' values showed under the card's words; the checker passed it because `text-contrast` measures the surface, not what shows through. F2, the card landed on the named field's neighbours and cut their labels. F3, the camera cut the side menu and the top bar. All three seen 5 October 2026 in a fresh starter video (engine 0.4.0) by the `video-frames` skill looking at each still at full size; fixed in roadmap step 8a: an opaque panel and the `surface-opaque` checker row; card placement clear of neighbours in engine 0.4.1; the starter's part 2 frame measured past the chrome.
