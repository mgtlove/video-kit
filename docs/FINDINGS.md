# Findings

A finding leaves the open list in the commit that fixes it and goes to the closed list with the step that fixed it. The `video-frames` skill writes here when it sees something the checker does not measure.

## Closed
- F9, one still in about three check runs differed between two browser sessions: 31 pixels by up to 11 levels inside the list page's refresh icon, seen 9 October 2026 on the S3 test video. Two causes, both measured: a crop from the 2x capture drawn at 18 by 21.5 CSS px (a half-pixel edge resampled differently per session), and, once the pictures were whole, an anti-aliased CSS rounded corner under a fractional camera scale mid move (12 pixels by 17 levels on the Upload button's end). Fixed the same day: `vkit app crop` grows a region to whole screen pixels at the capture's scale and says so, the S3 app's fifteen odd crops were re-cut and every picture is drawn at a whole size and place (`fidelity.md`); and the `deterministic` row holds the two sessions to the seek proof's tolerance (under 0.05 percent of pixels over 8 levels) and names each still that differed and by how much, because a browser does not promise identical anti-aliasing of a curve under a transform between sessions. Proof: five quick checks running, the row passing, the worst still 9 pixels over 8 levels.
