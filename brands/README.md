# Brands

One folder per brand: `brand.json` and the files it names (a mark image, say). `vkit brand <name>` copies the folder into a video's `rig/brand/` and writes `rig/brand.css` as `--brand-*` tokens; `theme.css` reads those before the look and before its own defaults, so a brand wins where it speaks and is silent where it does not, and a video with no brand renders byte for byte the same (`brand.test.js`). `vkit brand none` empties the file; `vkit brand` with no name rebuilds it after a hand edit of `rig/brand/brand.json`; `vkit brand --check` reports what is applied and each colour against the surface it sits on, by the contrast rules in `rules.json`.

`brand.json` holds five colours (ground, ink, primary, secondary, highlight; any may be empty), two faces (display for headings, labels and the mark; text for sentences and card bodies; system stacks until a face has a licence row, C-TYPE-15), a mark and a banner. The mark has `text` and or a square image `file`, and both the mark and the banner are placed by `when` (never, opener, close, both, always, watermark), `where` (a corner for the mark; top or bottom for the banner), `size` (pixels high), `opacity` and `seconds` (how long the opener and the close last). `always` hides while a recreated screen is up so the product is never covered; `watermark` stays. Every placement is inside title safe and above the caption band; that is the engine's, not the brand's.

`example/` is a neutral brand that shows the shape and passes `vkit check` on the starter. `TEMPLATE/` is the empty shape to copy. Nothing here is anyone's real brand.
